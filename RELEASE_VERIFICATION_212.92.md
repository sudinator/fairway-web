## Approved scope and observable behavior

- Club play has Last 30 days / Last 90 days / Year to date. Dates are inclusive US Eastern calendar dates; YTD begins January 1 in the current Eastern year. App-open counts remain separately labelled as 30-day app-wide use.
- Aggregate additions: repeat players with two or more distinct qualifying rounds; formats ranked by completed club-game count; recorded holes; gross eagles-or-better, birdies, pars, bogeys and doubles-or-worse. No personal names, IDs, performance rankings or individual results are returned by the aggregate endpoint.
- Existing qualifying participation/course rules are preserved: current active non-support members, valid finished game cards and finalized club-linked personal rounds; no guests/no-shows, unfinished/deleted/foreign/out-of-period entries, duplicate game/user entries or posted game copies. Personal rounds have no game format. Shared-ball participation counts each eligible member; shared gross holes count once per game/foursome/side. Canonical rows including null tombstones win. Legacy side fallback for aggregate holes uses the first valid eligible non-no-show player card in stable player-ID order, rather than the database's incidental row order.
- Holes require a positive integer recorded stroke score. Missing/invalid par does not remove the recorded hole from the hole total, but excludes it from classification and is disclosed. Score classification reuses existing golf.holeBuckets; no private replacement scoring engine. Gross classifications do not calculate net or handicap outcomes.
- Expand/collapse remains inline, preserving selected section, period and directory state. Club switching resets hub state. No Activity tab. Existing published-report view/cadence remains unchanged. No monthly report schedule is added.
- Remove the redundant top Invite members shortcut. Real invitation controls remain under Club settings. Member phone numbers, name-to-profile, Call/Text/Say hi and all club-admin permissions remain. Club name remains explicitly cream.
- Owner selected the shorter one-line dashboard wording after the full original sentence failed narrow-width measurement: “Stats: 5 rounds · WHS index: best 8 of 20”. Count/singular change with selected window. Sage, 11px, nowrap. No dashboard selection, statistic, index computation, callback or save behavior changes.

Full-source candidate based on 212.93. No live database, staging, email or production changes occurred here. Not production-release approval.

## Approved behavior

The expanded club scoring section shows Gross and Net side by side. Each column has its own eligible-hole denominator and percentages. Categories are Eagles+, Birdies, Pars, Bogeys and Doubles+. Zero eligible holes shows dashes rather than invented zero performance. Missing handicap and incomplete stroke-allocation exclusions are counted and explained. Missing par remains excluded from both columns. Club totals only; no personal identities or performance rankings are returned.

Individual game statistics use the existing full course-handicap basis, independent of opponent-relative strokes or a game's individual match allowance. Manual overrides are authoritative, including explicit scratch and nine-hole values. Derived game nines use the existing one-time halving. Historical personal rounds use their stored played-hole handicap snapshot without another halving; absent snapshots may derive from the historical round's own rating/slope/index/par, never today's profile.

Shared cards count each side's holes once. Alternate Shot reuses the existing saved allowance and side-handicap helper, with every partner's handicap required. Scramble reuses the existing team-handicap helper: USGA/custom weights require known partner bases; agreed handicaps require a finite explicit value (0 is valid); an explicitly gross game deliberately has zero adjustment. An unknown partner/agreed handicap is excluded from Net rather than silently treated as scratch. Guest teammates may contribute to a known team handicap, but the prior club participation/gross inclusion rules remain unchanged.

Net strokes are gross less the existing signed allocateStrokes allocation. Plus handicaps give strokes back. Zero and negative net outcomes remain eligible. Nonzero handicaps require complete, unique, valid stroke-index and hole metadata; partial personal metadata is not allowed to distribute a full-course handicap only across the scored portion. Known scratch needs no stroke-index allocation. The original gross inclusion rules, participation, repeat players, courses, formats, date ranges, member total, expand/collapse and dashboard remain unchanged.

Before you start

Use your testing club and throwaway games/rounds. Two devices signed in as Amit can check storage and reset behavior. A separate real staging login is required to prove non-marker permissions; synthetic player names do not provide a login.

Fresh reopen: fully close/reopen the installed app or force a genuine reload. A quick phone lock may keep the screen in memory and give a false pass. Pre-open the game online before offline tests so it has a saved snapshot.

Report each test as PASS, FAIL or BLOCKED. Any unexpected data loss, overwrite or unauthorized change means stop and report the exact steps. Do not mark an unavailable account, control or timing condition as PASS.

Results stay on this browser when storage is available. Download results to keep or share them. Nothing is sent to an app or server.

A1 · Deployment gates

  2. Confirm the fresh-database rebuild, live staging migration parity, local CI and Vercel deployment are green.
  3. Confirm migration 0161 is applied to staging and the app reports 192.20.

EXPECT: All gates green. 192.20 has no new migration; 0161 is still required. If CI fails, report the new “Fresh database failure diagnostics” section and stop manual testing.

B1 · Online save and independent refresh

Required
  1. Create a throwaway own-ball game named “19220 QA Recovery” in your testing club.
  2. While online, enter scores 5 and 6 on two holes. Wait until no pending changes remain.
  3. Refresh from a second device/browser signed into staging.

EXPECT: The independently loaded scorecard shows 5 and 6. Totals reflect the entered values. Establish this baseline before going offline.

B2 · Offline correction, deletion and cold reopen

Required
  1. On the original device, turn off its connection (airplane mode, with Wi-Fi off).
  2. Change the first score from 5 to 4 and delete the second score.
  3. Fully close/reopen the installed app, or force a genuine reload while offline. Reopen the same game.
  4. Reconnect. Wait for pending changes to clear, then refresh the second device.

EXPECT: Offline, the first score remains 4 and the second stays blank. After reconnection, an independent refresh shows exactly 4 and blank. No reappearance of 5 or 6; totals update accordingly. A quick lock without reloading is not proof of recovery.

B3 · All peripheral stats, including zero/false

Required
  1. Online, enter a score plus putts 3, fairway miss, penalties 1 and sand yes. Wait for sync.
  2. Offline, change to putts 2, fairway hit, penalties 0 and sand no.
  3. Force a genuine reopen; then reconnect and refresh independently.
  4. Also clear a stat where the UI supports blank values and repeat reopen/reconnect.

EXPECT: Every changed stat survives reopen and appears after independent refresh. Zero penalties, sand no and deliberate blank values must not revert to their old values. Gross score remains unchanged.

B4 · Stats on an unscored hole

If supported by entry UI
  1. Leave one hole’s gross score blank. Enter a peripheral stat on that hole while offline.
  2. Reopen, reconnect and independently refresh. If the UI does not permit this action, record BLOCKED / not exposed.

EXPECT: The stat survives and syncs even without a gross score. Do not mark PASS if the action could not be exercised; the developer regression covers the underlying helper path.

B5 · Fast edits, screen lock and retry

Required
  1. On a test hole, enter 5 → 4 → 3 in quick succession.
  2. Immediately lock/background the phone, then force a genuine reopen.
  3. Repeat while offline, then reconnect. If pending changes remain, use “Sync now” once.

EXPECT: The final value 3 survives and is confirmed on independent refresh. Earlier 5/4 values do not return. While disconnected or saving, the app must not falsely claim all edits are synced.

C1 · Marker recovers another player’s scores

Required
  1. Use a throwaway group game with synthetic players and claim the group scorecard as marker.
  2. Save a score for another player online. Offline, correct that score and delete a different entered score for that player.
  3. Reopen offline, reconnect and independently refresh.

EXPECT: The other player’s latest values persist, including the deletion. Correct totals and scorecard values agree. A synthetic player is sufficient for this marker-side test.

C2 · Non-marker keeps stats; marker keeps gross

Needs a second genuine login
  1. Use a second staging account that is a player in the same game; synthetic names without credentials do not qualify.
  2. Keep Amit as marker. On the other player’s account, edit their own peripheral stats and reconnect/reopen.
  3. Confirm the marker’s gross score remains unchanged. If no second authenticated account is available, record BLOCKED.

EXPECT: Peripheral stats persist without replacing the marker’s gross score. Two devices signed in as Amit cannot prove this permission boundary. Local injected tests are developer evidence, not a substitute for this live account test.

D1 · Offline stale scores after organizer reset

Required; two devices can use Amit’s login
  1. Open the same throwaway game on devices A and B. On B, go offline and leave an unsynced edit.
  2. On online device A, reset the game’s scores as organizer.
  3. Reconnect B and reopen the game. Then enter one genuinely new score and sync it.
  4. Independently refresh A.

EXPECT: The pre-reset offline edit stays cleared. The new post-reset score persists. This tests reconnect replay after reset; it does not prove safety for a different device’s write already in flight at reset.

D2 · Delayed same-device write followed by reset

Developer-assisted diagnostic
  1. On the organizer’s device, delay a score request using controlled network throttling.
  2. Invoke Reset while that device’s write is in flight; release the delay.
  3. After reset completes, independently refresh the scorecard. Record BLOCKED if delay cannot be controlled.

EXPECT: The admitted same-device write completes before reset, and cleared scores do not return later. Uncontrolled timing or a normal fast connection does not prove this case.

E1 · Front-nine manual handicap: Finish group

Required
  1. Create “19220 QA Front 8”: front nine, own-ball format, your manual course handicap 8.
  2. Enter all nine scores so completion is not blocked by missing play. Claim the group marker role if needed.
  3. Finish the group; open the posted round in your history.
  4. End the game afterward and reopen that round.

EXPECT: The posted round retains course handicap 8 with manual source. Reposting through End game does not halve it to 4 or create a second round. Compare COURSE handicap, not the format’s playing handicap or relative stroke dots.

E2 · Back-nine manual handicap: End game

Required
  1. Create “19220 QA Back 8”: back nine, own-ball format, manual course handicap 8.
  2. Score all nine holes and End game as organizer.
  3. Open the posted round, refresh and reopen.

EXPECT: Course handicap is 8/manual. Recorded holes retain their real back-nine numbers 10–18. Gross score matches entered strokes; par matches the chosen nine. Slope is not halved.

E3 · Manual zero and plus handicap

Required
  1. Repeat a nine-hole posting with manual handicap 0.
  2. Repeat with a plus handicap, such as stored value -2 (displayed as +2 where the app uses golf notation).
  3. Check the posted round and refresh.

EXPECT: Zero stays 0/manual; the plus handicap stays the same signed value/manual. Neither falls back to a derived figure. If the UI will not accept the intended signed value, report that as a failure rather than changing the test.

E4 · Derived control and 18-hole control

Required
  1. Create a nine-hole game with no manual override; record the saved player handicap before posting.
  2. Post and inspect the derived round. Developer/database verification should compare it with rounded half of the saved game-player figure.
  3. Repeat ordinary 18-hole posting, including a manual override and ordinary derived handicap.

EXPECT: Nine-hole derived scaling retains the prior rule; 18-hole figures are not halved. Source remains derived/manual as appropriate. Avoid comparing a format-adjusted playing handicap with the posted course handicap.

F1 · Personal-round Save and Cancel regression

Required
  1. Create a new personal round, enter scores, save/finish and refresh its history entry.
  2. Open a completed round, change a score, wait briefly, then Cancel and reopen.
  3. Repeat the edit and use Save changes.

EXPECT: New Save succeeds without 22P02 and creates one round. Cancel restores the original stored score; explicit Save changes persists the new score after refresh.

F2 · Alternate Shot and normal completion

Required
  1. Finish a throwaway Alternate Shot game and inspect individual history.
  2. On a throwaway own-ball game, leave unsynced edits and try Finish/End; then reconnect, sync and finish.

EXPECT: Alternate Shot does not post a shared-ball score as an individual round. Own-ball completion does not proceed as though pending edits were already stored. Existing completion prompts and flows remain usable.

Open concurrency risk — keep separate

INFERENCE, not a reproduced staging finding: a write already in flight on a different device can race a server reset because current writes do not require a reset version. D1 checks stale offline replay after reconnect; D2 checks same-device ordering. Neither proves a cross-device transaction guarantee. This stays on the audit list.

Legacy backups without a confirmed watermark can recover missing data conservatively. Previously lost edits cannot be reconstructed. Migration 0161 does not automatically repair historical posted rounds.
